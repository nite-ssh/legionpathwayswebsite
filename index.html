What we do

Legion Pathways runs conversion rate optimization (CRO) and UX testing programs for DTC e-commerce brands. Every layout, copy, or checkout change ships as a controlled test against live traffic first, so the lift shows up in the data before it rolls out sitewide.

Checkout friction, cluttered navigation, and slow product pages drain visitors after the ad spend already converted them into a click. CRO targets that exact gap: the funnel between arrival and purchase.

Four stages, first site review to measured conversion lift. Every stage produces a deliverable, not just a status update.

01
Audit

Site architecture, user behavior data, and conversion bottlenecks get mapped across the full buyer journey, landing page through checkout.

02
Hypothesize

Layout, design, and flow ideas get ranked by expected lift before any get built, cutting low-impact ideas at the planning stage.

03
Test

Structured A/B tests run across key landing and product pages, measured against a fixed baseline set before launch.

04
Scale

Winning variants roll out sitewide with full documentation, so your team can maintain and iterate on the build independently.

About Legion Pathways

We test what works. We don't guess.

Legion Pathways is a conversion rate optimization (CRO) and UX partner for DTC e-commerce brands. Audits, structured A/B testing, and fixes you own outright, delivered as tested results.

Our model

The gap isn't traffic. It's what happens after the click.

Most e-commerce brands have already invested in ads, SEO, or influencer partnerships to drive traffic. Testing what happens after arrival gets skipped, along with the judgment to know which layout change is actually worth shipping.

Legion Pathways runs both halves: audit the funnel to locate the exact drop-off point, then validate the fix with a structured A/B test before it goes live sitewide.

Clients approve test priorities and review the resulting lift in the numbers. Running the tests, reading the statistics, and learning a new platform stays on our side.

Test plans, winning variants, and full documentation transfer at the end of every engagement. The gains stay on the site regardless of what happens next.
