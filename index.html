About Legion Pathways

We track what works. We find who can build it.

Legion Pathways is not an AI agency selling strategy, and not a tool reseller. We are an implementation partner for DTC brands — systems installed, operators sourced, results owned by you.

Our model

The gap isn't ambition. It's implementation.

Almost every DTC founder we speak to already knows AI should be doing more in their business. What's missing is the person who can actually build it into their stack — and the judgement to know which of this week's tools will still matter next quarter.

So we do both. We test what's genuinely working across e-commerce operations and throw out what doesn't survive contact with a real store. Then we match the work to operators and technical consultants who have shipped it before.

You don't manage the build, vet the freelancers, or learn a new platform to review progress. You approve the priorities and receive working systems.

Everything we install is documented and handed over. If we disappear tomorrow, your systems keep running — that's the standard we build to.
