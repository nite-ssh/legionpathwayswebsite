FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does your commission model work?
Legion Pathways operates on a performance-driven, commission-based model. Specific terms are discussed and agreed upon with each brand during the discovery process.
What kinds of brands do you work with?
We work primarily with supplement, nutraceutical, wellness, functional food and beverage, and consumer packaged goods brands that already have a commercially viable product and want to expand into physical retail.
Do you guarantee retail placements?
No. Retail placement depends on many factors, including product fit, category, pricing, and retailer needs. We focus on disciplined outreach and follow-up to create opportunities, but we do not guarantee specific placements or outcomes.
How long does retail expansion take?
Timelines vary by brand, category, and retailer sales cycle. Most engagements begin with strategy and targeting in the first few weeks, followed by buyer outreach and relationship development in the following months.
Do you work nationwide?
We work with brands across the United States and evaluate territory and market fit as part of the brand assessment process.
What retailers do you target?
Retailer targets are determined based on your product category, positioning, and growth goals. We identify the retail channels most likely to be a fit rather than pursuing a fixed list of accounts.
Can early-stage brands apply?
We typically work with brands that already have a commercially viable product and some traction. During a discovery call, we can assess whether Legion Pathways is a good fit for your current stage.
What happens after the first order?
We continue to manage the buyer relationship, support communication, and identify opportunities for reorders and expanded distribution within that account.
