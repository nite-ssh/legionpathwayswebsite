Four stages from first look at your site to a conversion lift you can measure. No redesign for the sake of a redesign.

Audit

02

About Legion Pathways

We test what works. We don't guess.

Legion Pathways is a conversion rate optimization and UX partner for DTC e-commerce brands — audits, structured testing, and fixes you own, not a redesign pitch.

Our model

The gap isn't traffic. It's what happens after the click.

Almost every e-commerce brand we talk to has already spent on ads, SEO, or influencers to get people to the site. What's missing is the discipline to test what happens once they land — and the judgement to know which change is worth shipping.

So we run both sides. We audit the funnel to find where visitors actually drop off, then validate every fix with a structured A/B test before it goes live sitewide.

You don't run the tests, read the statistics, or learn a new testing platform to review results. You approve the priorities and see the lift in the numbers.

Everything we build — test plans, winning variants, documentation — is handed over. If we stop working together tomorrow, your site keeps the gains.
