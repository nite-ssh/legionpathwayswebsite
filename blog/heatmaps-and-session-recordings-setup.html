How to Set Up Heatmaps and Session Recordings the Right Way

By Nitesh Poudel · 6 min read
Illustration: A heatmap showing click density

Heatmaps and session recordings are some of the most under-used tools in e-commerce, mostly because they're installed once and then never actually watched with a specific question in mind.

Set up with a question, not just a tool

Installing a recording tool without a specific question — "why is checkout drop-off high" or "are people finding the size guide" — leads to hours of unfocused watching. Define what you're trying to learn before you start reviewing sessions.

Filter before you watch

Watching random sessions wastes time. Filter recordings to the specific page and the specific behavior that matters — sessions that reached checkout but didn't convert, for example — so every session you watch is relevant to the question you're asking.

Ten to fifteen sessions is usually enough to see a pattern

You don't need to watch hundreds of recordings. A clear, repeated pattern across ten to fifteen relevant sessions is usually enough to form a confident hypothesis worth testing.

Respect privacy from the start

Mask sensitive fields (payment details, personal information) at the tool level before you start recording, not as an afterthought.

Key takeaways

  • Start with a specific question, not just "let's see what happens."
  • Filter recordings to the relevant page and behavior before watching.
  • Ten to fifteen relevant sessions is usually enough to spot a real pattern.
